How it works

Group stage

Group sizes vary by division because entry numbers did. Every team is guaranteed at least four matches across the day.

Ranking a group

Ranked on matches won. Two teams level — the winner of their match ranks higher. Three or more level — a team that has beaten every other tied team ranks highest, and the rest are re-ranked the same way.

Still level

Game difference in the matches between the tied teams only, then games won in those matches, then a draw made by the Tournament Director with a player from each team present.

Repechage

In Men's A/B and Women's A/B the fourth semifinal place is not decided in the groups. The four teams that miss out play it off, so a bad start does not end your day.

Medals

Champion and runner-up in each of the six divisions. No third-place medal.

Court rules

Nets at 1.70 m on every court in every division. A 3 m prohibited zone applies to all players. Otherwise the 2026 ITF Rules of Beach Tennis apply.

Self-officiated

Players call their own matches. Bring scoring questions to the organizing table. Conduct matters go to the Tournament Director, David Garzon, on site or at vanbeachtennis@gmail.com.

Walkovers

A walkover or retirement is recorded as 6–0 so that group standings and game difference still work.
