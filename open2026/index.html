Rules in short

How long each match is
StageFormat
Group stageOne set to 6 · tiebreak at 6–6
Semifinals · 3rd / 4thOne set to 6 · tiebreak at 6–6
FinalsTwo sets to 6 · super tiebreak to 10 instead of a third set
RepechageOne set to 4 · tiebreak at 4–4
PlacementMixed C/D one set to 6 · Women's and Men's A/B one set to 4
Who finishes higher in a group
  1. Matches won.
  2. Head to head — two teams level, the winner of their match goes above.
  3. Game difference in the matches between the tied teams only.
  4. Games won in those same matches.
  5. Draw by the Tournament Director, with a player from each team there.
